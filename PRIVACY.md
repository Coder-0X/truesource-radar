# Privacy Policy — TrueSource Radar

> Last Updated: October 25, 2026

TrueSource Radar is committed to user privacy. We operate under a strict **Zero Personal Data Collection Policy**.

---

### 1. No Personal Data Collected
TrueSource Radar does **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit:
- Personally identifiable information (PII)
- Browsing history or visited URLs
- Passwords, cookies, or account credentials
- Financial data or payment information

### 2. Local-Only Processing
All product evaluation, price parsing, and image hashing happen **100% locally on your computer** within your browser. Scanned history and settings are stored strictly in local device storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and can be cleared at any time by clicking "Clear" in the popup.

### 3. Outbound Search Links
Clicking direct links (AliExpress, 1688, Alibaba, Bing Visual Search, Google Lens) simply opens a search query in a new tab.

### 4. Regulatory & Store Compliance
This policy fulfills all requirements set forth in:
- **Microsoft Store App Developer Agreement (Section 4.h)**
- **Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies**
- **GDPR & CCPA**

### 5. Contact
For questions or concerns, please open an issue on GitHub.
