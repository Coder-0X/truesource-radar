Privacy Policy for TrueSource Radar

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

TrueSource Radar ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension designed to provide pricing transparency and discover factory wholesale sources for products browsed online. We take your privacy very seriously and operate under a strict zero personal data collection policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

TrueSource Radar does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of the following:

  • No personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, address, or phone number.
  • No browsing history, bookmarks, or list of visited websites.
  • No financial data, payment details, passwords, or login credentials.
  • No IP addresses or persistent device identifiers.

2. How the Extension Works Locally

All product analysis, price comparisons, and image hashing are performed 100% locally on your device inside your browser window:

  • When you view an Amazon, Etsy, or supported shopping page, the extension inspects the publicly visible product title, price, and image in the current tab.
  • This metadata is processed client-side to calculate origin risk scores and format direct search URLs.
  • Scan history and user preferences (such as auto-scan settings) are saved solely on your local device using standard browser storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never sent to external servers.

3. Outbound Links & Third-Party Services

When you click on search action buttons (e.g., AliExpress, 1688, Alibaba, Bing Visual Search, or Google Lens), the extension opens a standard search link in a new browser tab. Your interaction with those third-party websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

5. Compliance with Store Policies

This policy complies with the Microsoft Store App Developer Agreement (Section 4.h), the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA regulations.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, you can contact the developer via GitHub Issues or by email at: support@truesource.local
